KINDER GYM PROGRAM

Building Foundations Through Play | Ages 2.5-5.5

Ages 2.5 - 5.5 years
Duration 45 minutes
Class Size 8-10 students

Your child's first steps into gymnastics start here. Our Kinder Gym program is specially designed for preschoolers, introducing the fundamentals of gymnastics through fun, age-appropriate activities that feel like play.

We understand that every child develops at their own pace. Our patient, enthusiastic coaches create a nurturing environment where your little one can explore movement, build confidence, and discover what their body can do—all while having the time of their lives.

What Your Child Will Learn

Through carefully designed activities and games, we focus on:

🏃 Motor Skills

Running, jumping, rolling, climbing, and balancing—the building blocks of all movement.

🧘 Body Awareness

Understanding where their body is in space and how to control it.

👥 Social Skills

Following directions, taking turns, and interacting positively with other kids.

Physical Strength

Core strength, flexibility, and coordination developed through play.

Confidence

"I did it!" moments that build self-esteem and independence.

Focus

Attention span, patience, and ability to follow multi-step directions.

Class Structure

Each 45-minute class follows a fun, predictable structure that helps kids feel comfortable and engaged:

  • Warm-Up Circle (5-10 minutes): Fun games and stretches to get bodies ready
  • Skill Stations (30-35 minutes): Rotating through apparatus with age-appropriate activities on bars, beam, vault, and floor
  • Cool Down & Game (5-10 minutes): Parachute play, obstacle courses, or movement games

Why Gymnastics for Preschoolers?

Gymnastics provides the perfect foundation for all physical activities. Whether your child continues with gymnastics or moves to soccer, dance, swimming, or any other sport, the body awareness, coordination, and confidence they develop here will serve them for life.

Beyond physical skills, our program helps kids develop focus and attention span, the ability to follow multi-step directions, patience and perseverance, respect for others and coaches, and joy in movement and physical activity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can parents watch classes? +

Yes! We have a comfortable viewing area. Some younger kids focus better without parents in the gym, but you're always welcome to observe.

What should my child wear? +

Comfortable athletic wear that allows full range of motion. Leotards, athletic shorts, and fitted t-shirts work great. No jeans or loose clothing. Bare feet or gymnastics shoes only—no socks.

What if my child is shy or nervous? +

Our coaches are experts at helping nervous kids feel comfortable. We go at their pace, never forcing participation. Most kids warm up within 2-3 classes!

Do you offer makeup classes? +

Yes! If your child misses a class, you can schedule a makeup class through the customer portal (subject to availability).
