Common Questions

MEMBERSHIP FAQ

Can I upgrade or downgrade my tier? +

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade anytime through your customer portal. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. This gives you total flexibility to adjust as your schedule or goals change.

What are bonus classes and how do they work? +

Bonus classes are extra classes you can use each month for makeup classes or additional practice. Kickoff gets 1 bonus class/month, Platinum and Ultimate get 2 bonus classes/month. Important: They DON'T roll over—use them or lose them! To use a bonus class, find an available class in the portal and email info@californiastrongathletics.com to request registration.

Can I mix programs (like Rec + Tumbling)? +

Absolutely! Since Rec and Tumbling are priced the same, you can use one Platinum membership ($185/month) for 1 Rec class + 1 Tumbling class per week. This is more cost-effective than two separate memberships and gives your child variety!

Is there a sibling discount? +

Yes! You get a 20% discount on the second child (and additional siblings). We can also help coordinate overlapping class times so both kids are at the gym together. Email info@californiastrongathletics.com to arrange this.

What's the 30-day conditional trial? +

Enroll, attend all your scheduled classes for 30 days (4-5 classes), and if your child isn't absolutely loving it, we'll refund your tuition. The only risk? The $75 annual insurance fee (non-refundable). The catch: you must attend ALL classes—one class isn't enough to know if gymnastics is the right fit!

How long are the classes? +

KinderGym: 45 minutes of play-based learning
Rec Level 1/2: 70 minutes
Rec Level 3/4: 90 minutes
Tumbling: 60 minutes

We have the longest class times in the Bay Area—more time means better skill development!

How do I cancel my membership? +

Log into your portal or email info@californiastrongathletics.com before the last day of the month to avoid being charged for the next month. Our memberships are flexible month-to-month with no long-term contracts. If you cancel on the 1st or later, you're charged for that month (no refunds, but classes remain available).

What's included in the $75 insurance fee? +

The $75 annual insurance fee covers liability insurance and safety equipment for the year. It's a one-time charge per year (not monthly). This is required for all students and is non-refundable.
