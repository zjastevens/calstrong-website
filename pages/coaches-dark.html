MEET OUR COACHES

Heart, expertise, and a genuine love for teaching kids

At Cal Strong, we hire coaches who bring more than just gymnastics expertise—they bring heart. Every coach is USAG-certified, CPR-trained, and background-checked. But what really sets them apart is how they show up for your child every day: with patience, positivity, and a genuine belief in what every athlete can achieve.
