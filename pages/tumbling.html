TUMBLING PROGRAM

Flips, Tricks & Acrobatic Skills | Ages 5.5-16 | Coed

An action-packed journey into the world of flips, twists, and thrilling tumbles. Perfect for cheerleaders, dancers, martial artists, and thrill-seekers ready to discover their inner acrobat.

Our tumbling program focuses exclusively on floor skills—no beams, bars, or vault. Just pure tumbling progression from basic rolls to advanced aerial skills. Whether you're a cheerleader looking to level up, a dancer adding acrobatics, or someone who just wants to learn to flip, this is your class.

Skills You'll Learn

  • Foundation Skills: Rolls, cartwheels, roundoffs, handstands
  • Intermediate Skills: Front & back handsprings, aerials, walkovers
  • Advanced Skills: Standing & running tucks, layouts, fulls
  • Specialty Skills: Aerials, side somis, twisting combinations
  • Combinations: Connecting skills into powerful tumbling passes

Perfect For

This coed program is ideal for:

  • Cheerleaders preparing for competition season
  • Dancers adding acrobatic elements to routines
  • Martial artists building aerial kick skills
  • Athletes cross-training for other sports
  • Anyone who dreams of learning to flip!
