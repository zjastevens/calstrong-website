Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know to get started

How do I know which program is right for my child? +

Use our Class Finder tool above, or give us a call at (925) 826-5690. We'll ask about your child's age, experience level, and interests to recommend the perfect program. You can also schedule a free trial class to see which program they enjoy most!

What should my child wear to class? +

For girls: a leotard or fitted athletic wear (no loose clothing). For boys: athletic shorts or pants and a fitted t-shirt. No shoes required — we train barefoot! Hair should be pulled back securely. No jewelry or accessories for safety.

How much does it cost? +

Our recreational programs range from $120-$300/month depending on the tier (Starter, Platinum, or Ultimate). All memberships include bonus classes, unlimited summer/holiday training, and skill tracking. We offer a 20% sibling discount and 10% discount for annual payment. First trial class is always free!

Can I watch my child's class? +

Yes! We have a comfortable viewing area where parents can watch. For our youngest students (Kinder Gym), we recommend staying for the first few classes until your child is comfortable. Older students often prefer independence, but you're always welcome to watch.

What if my child has never done gymnastics before? +

Perfect! Most of our students start with zero experience. Our coaches are trained to work with beginners and will meet your child exactly where they are. We use age-appropriate progressions to build skills safely and confidently from day one.

How do I schedule a trial class? +

Click any "Start Your Free Trial" button on this page, or call us directly at (925) 826-5690. We'll find a class time that works for your schedule and get you registered. No commitment required for your trial class!

What is your safety and coaching certification? +

All our coaches are USAG (USA Gymnastics) certified and background checked. We maintain a 1:8-10 coach-to-student ratio, use professional equipment, and follow strict safety protocols. Our facility is fully insured and we prioritize safety above all else.

Do you offer competitive gymnastics teams? +

Yes! Our competitive team program is by invitation only after evaluation by our coaching staff. We compete in the USAG Junior Olympic program at levels 1-10. Contact us if you're interested in a team evaluation for your child.

What is your cancellation/refund policy? +

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all memberships. If you're not completely satisfied within the first month, we'll refund your tuition, no questions asked. After that, memberships can be cancelled with 30 days notice.
