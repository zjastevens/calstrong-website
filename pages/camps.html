What to Expect

Daily Schedule

9:00 AM - 3:00 PM with extended care available. Structured gym time, skill stations, games, and free play.

Ages & Groups

Ages 5-12 grouped by age and skill level. Beginner-friendly—no experience required!

What's Included

USAG-certified coaching, all equipment, snacks, and daily themed activities. Pack a lunch!

Pricing

$60-75 per day. Multi-day discounts available. Early bird pricing when you sign up 3+ weeks in advance.

What They Learn

Skills on all apparatus, strength & flexibility training, teamwork, and confidence building.

What to Bring

Lunch, water bottle, athletic clothes, and a smile! We provide snacks and all equipment.
