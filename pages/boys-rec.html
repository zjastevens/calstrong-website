BOYS RECREATIONAL GYMNASTICS

Building Strength, Confidence & Athletic Excellence | Ages 5.5-14

Ages5.5 - 14 years
Duration70-90 minutes
LevelsBeginner to Advanced

Real gymnastics training for boys who want to challenge themselves, build serious athletic skills, and have fun doing it. Our boys recreational program teaches progressive skills on all six men's Olympic apparatus using USAG-approved progressions.

Whether your son is brand new to gymnastics or has been flipping since he could walk, we have a class for him. Our structured skill progression system ensures that every athlete is challenged at the right level while building a rock-solid foundation of strength, flexibility, and body control.

The Six Olympic Apparatus

Floor Exercise

Tumbling passes, acrobatic skills, and dynamic movement combinations.

Pommel Horse

Circles, scissors, and advanced strength & coordination skills.

Rings

Strength holds, swings, and dismounts that build incredible upper body power.

Vault

Explosive power and precise technique for spectacular vaults.

Parallel Bars

Swinging skills, strength elements, and dynamic transitions.

High Bar

Release moves, giants, and spectacular dismounts.

What Your Son Will Develop

  • Full-Body Strength: Core, upper body, and functional power that translates to every sport
  • Mental Toughness: Overcoming fear, pushing limits, and building resilience
  • Body Control: Spatial awareness and coordination that creates confident athletes
  • Discipline & Focus: Following progressions, respecting coaches, and setting goals
  • Confidence: "I can do hard things" mentality that carries into life

Level Progression System

We use a structured 4-level system that ensures proper skill development:

  • Level 1: Beginner fundamentals and basic skills on all apparatus
  • Level 2: Intermediate skills with increased difficulty and combinations
  • Level 3: Advanced recreational with complex skills and routines
  • Level 4: Pre-competitive preparation for athletes considering team
